### 1. AI-Driven Content Factory (Social Media Growth)
Students are drawn to "aesthetic" spots. You don't need a professional agency; you can use AI to create a high-end visual brand that looks like you spent lakhs on marketing.

### 2. AI-Powered "Instant Response" Chatbot
Students hate waiting. If they DM you asking "Do you have Wi-Fi?" or "What's your menu?" and you reply 4 hours later, you've lost them to another cafe.

*   **How it works:** Set up **ManyChat** (integrated with Instagram and WhatsApp). It uses AI-driven automation to answer FAQs instantly, send your digital menu, and even offer a "First-timer discount code" automatically when someone messages you.
*   **Estimated Monthly Cost:** **PKR 2,500** (Basic paid plan for automation).
*   **First Step:** Map out your top 5 most asked questions (e.g., Location, Menu, Wi-Fi, Timing) and set up "Keyword Triggers" in ManyChat so the AI replies instantly.

Instead of spending money on broad ads, use Meta’s (Facebook/Instagram) AI-driven "Advantage+" targeting to show your ads *only* to students within a 2-3km radius of your cafe during specific hours (e.g., 4 PM to 9 PM).

*   **Estimated Monthly Cost:** **PKR 5,000** (Daily spend of ~PKR 160–170).
*   **First Step:** Create a simple "Student Offer" graphic and launch a "Reach" campaign on Meta Ads Manager, setting the location pin strictly around your neighborhood and targeting ages 18–24.
